FAQ

The questions everyone asks first.

How is ad spend charged?

Ad spend goes directly from your card to Google / Meta — we never touch it and never mark it up. Our monthly fee is for the strategy, account management and reporting. You see exactly what's being spent and where.

What's the minimum ad budget?

We recommend £750/mo minimum on Launch, £1,500/mo on Growth and £3,000/mo on Scale. Below those amounts campaigns don't have enough data to learn from and you're paying us more than you're paying Google.

How fast can campaigns go live?

Seven working days from sign-off, in most cases. The setup phase (week one) covers account access, conversion tracking, audience research and creative briefs. Campaigns go live in week two.

Do I have to use your SEO platform?

No. Digital Marketing stands on its own and can be the only thing you take from us. That said, customers on a Starter / Agency / Agency Pro plan get tighter integration — landing-page recommendations from their scan data and a single dashboard for everything.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel any time with 30 days' notice (we wind campaigns down responsibly, return account ownership cleanly and never hold your ad accounts hostage). Most agencies lock you in for 6–12 months. We don't.

Who actually runs the ads?

Our team — UK-based, paid-media specialists who've spent years running Google + Meta accounts. Not outsourced, not automated. You'll meet the person running your account on your discovery call.
